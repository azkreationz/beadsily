## 1. Quick Canva Brand Kit Setup Reference

When setting up or verifying the **BeadsILY Brand Kit** in Canva, use these exact parameters:

### Brand Colors (RGB / HEX):
| Swatch | Role in Canva | HEX Code | Notes |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| **Bubble Pink** | Primary Accent / CTA | `#FF689D` | Use for buttons, badges, and `ILY` in logo |
| **Soft Black** | Primary Copy / Outlines | `#171416` | Use for headings, body copy, and `BEADS` |
| **Pearl Cream** | Canvas Background | `#FFF8EF` | Primary warm background; never harsh pure white |
| **Soft Pink** | Card Fills / Shapes | `#FFC4DD` | Subtle background containers & organic blobs |
| **Warm Gold** | Stars & Accents | `#FFC107` | Stars, sparkles, and moon motifs |
| **Rose** | Shading & Accents | `#D93D75` | Accent borders and high-contrast text on cream |
| **Light Pink** | Highlights & Ears | `#FF97BD` | Cat ears and bead shine details |
| **Pearl Shade** | Neutral Borders | `#E7DECB` | Dividers, chip outlines, and card borders |
| **Crisp White** | Bead Highlights | `#FFFFFF` | Upper-left bead specular reflections only |

### Brand Fonts in Canva:
- **Display Headlines (Short & Punchy):** *Recoleta Bold* (or *Cinzel Bold* if Recoleta is not uploaded)
- **Section Labels & Accents:** *Cinzel* (All Caps, letter spacing set to `+150` or `+200`)
- **Body & Subtitles:** *Poppins Regular* (Line spacing `1.4` to `1.5`)
- **Buttons & Callouts:** *Poppins SemiBold* or *Bold*
