## 1. Positioning

An experience-led bead bar for playful self-expression: make a piece, pick a charm, and take home something that feels like you. Bead bar events, finished accessories, fancy beaded pens and take-home kits should feel like parts of the same welcoming boutique.

The visual balance is polished typography plus cheerful, tangible bead illustrations. The black wordmark lends clarity; pink creates recognition; cream and restrained gold keep the artwork warm. Cats, hearts, stars and moons provide variety within a shared drawing style.

### Wordmark Construction Rules
- The entire word is **BEADSILY**, with **BEADS** in soft black (`#171416`) and **ILY** in bubble pink (`#FF689D`).
- Keep it as **one uninterrupted name**.
- All letters after B share the same optical cap height. B is approximately 10% taller.
- The signature heart aligns over the I in the full logos.
- **Strict Prohibition:** Do not move the heart to another letter or recreate the logo by typing a font.
- The profile logo uses the same master as the primary, scaled proportionally. Its cream circle and fine pink ring are deliberate. There is no outer pink square. The bracelet submark reuses the same B and the same bead shapes.

## 3. Clear Space and Starting Minimum Sizes

Use at least half the wordmark cap height as free space around horizontal logos, measured from the outermost heart or bead. Around the icon and submark, allow at least one small pearl bead's diameter. Avoid placing type or borders inside this space.

### Suggested Starting Sizes (to confirm on actual material):
- **Detailed primary:** at least 65 mm wide in print, or 400 px on screen.
- **Secondary:** at least 30 mm wide, or 180 px on screen.
- **Detailed bracelet submark:** at least 30 mm wide, or 150 px on screen.
- **Simple B icon:** at least 10 mm wide, or 40 px on screen.
- At small avatar sizes use the B icon; the full bead strand will lose detail.

*Note: These are design guidelines, not a substitute for an output proof. Embroidery, engraving and vinyl cutting usually need a simplified application-specific adaptation.*

## 5. Typography

- **Logo:** Custom outlined display lettering. Use supplied vector files; no font dependency.
- **Labels and short headings:** **Cinzel**. Airy letter spacing works well for `BEAD BAR` and compact section labels.
- **Supporting copy:** **Poppins Regular**, with **Poppins Medium** for emphasis where available.
- **Optional display copy:** Recoleta Bold (subject to licensing). Use for short headlines rather than redrawing the logo.
- **Alternative body family:** Montserrat may replace Poppins in an application that already uses it; use one body family consistently within that application.

---

## 6. Illustration Rules

- Beads share rounded outlines, solid fills, a small upper-left white highlight and restrained flat shading.
- Keep highlights in the same direction.
- Use the supplied motifs at uniform sizes and scale all strokes with the artwork.
- **Strict Prohibition:** Do not mix these beads with photorealistic, gradient-heavy or differently outlined clip art.
- The main strand follows a shallow arc with matching outer bead sequences. The center contains a heart, flanked by the moon and confetti star. Cats, pearls and onyx beads balance the color. A visible string through open charm shapes is intentional.
- Keep supporting compositions simple: one focal charm and a few smaller accents are usually sufficient.
- On signage, the brand name takes priority over decorative motifs. Avoid adding a full bracelet, extra strand and scattered charms to the same small layout.

## 7. Voice & Campaign Directions

Friendly, expressive and inviting. Short, clear invitations work better than exaggerated luxury claims or childish language. Center the experience of choosing and making.

### Approved Campaign Lines:
- *“A little charm. A lot of heart.”*
- *“Pick your beads. Make it yours.”*
- *“Your next favorite little thing.”*

*(These are proposed campaign lines, not mandatory parts of the logo.)*

## 8. Application Strategy

1. **School Festival Booth (Oct 23, 2026):**
   - Lead with one large primary logo.
   - Clean price/service menu using Poppins.
   - Limited number of decorative motifs.
   - Secondary mark on smaller cards and packaging.
2. **Social Media & Favicons:**
   - Use cream profile for social pages.
   - Use B icon where the interface crops or renders the brand very small.
3. **Event Kits:**
   - Retain the same front label and vary one focal charm or accent arrangement to identify the collection.
4. **Product Photography:**
   - Product photos must use calm cream or white settings so the real bead colors remain the focus.
   - Keep the colorful illustrated strand as a brand signature, not a frame around every photograph.
