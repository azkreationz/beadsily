## 🎨 PROJECT 1: Your Beadable Pen

*Estimated Time: 10 minutes | Skill: Super Easy!*

1. **Unscrew:** Hold the pen body and gently twist the top round cap (finial) **counter-clockwise** to unscrew it. Place the cap in your bead tray.
2. **Choose Your Beads:** Pick **1 Theme Focal Bead** and **3 to 4 Accent Beads**.
3. **Slide & Stack:** Slide your beads onto the silver metal rod in any pattern you love.
   - *Pro Tip:* Put a small round spacer bead at the bottom and top so your big focal bead sits right in the middle!
4. **Screw It On:** Twist the top cap back on **clockwise** with your fingers until snug. (No need to squeeze too hard!)
5. **Write Away:** Twist the pen tip at the bottom to write. You just made your first project!

## 🎒 PROJECT 2: Backpack Charm / Keychain

*Estimated Time: 15 minutes | Skill: Easy & Fun!*

1. **Loop the Hardware:**
   - Fold your charm cord in half to make a loop at the top.
   - Push the loop through the eyelet of the lobster clasp.
   - Pull the two tail ends through the loop and tug firmly.
2. **String Over Both Strands:**
   - Pinch the two cord tails together.
   - Slide on 1 accent bead, followed by your Theme Focal bead and 3–4 colorful beads.
3. **Tie the Bottom Knot:**
   - Tie a simple double knot at the bottom of your last bead.
   - Pull both strands tight in opposite directions.
   - Ask your host or an adult to help trim the extra cord leaving a neat 1/4" tail.
4. **Clip & Go:** Clip your charm onto your backpack zipper, lunch tote, or key set!

## 💫 PROJECT 3: Stretch Keepsake Bracelet

*Estimated Time: 20 minutes | Skill: The Magic Knot!*

1. **The Secret Stretch:**
   - Take your 12" clear elastic cord.
   - Gently pull it 3 to 4 times with your hands. (This wakes up the cord so it won't stretch out later!)
2. **Design Your Pattern:**
   - Slide on your Theme Focal bead in the center.
   - Add spacer beads on each side. Want your name or initials? Add your letter beads now!
   - *Test your fit:* Wrap the bead strand around your wrist. For most crafters, 14–16 beads is the perfect fit.
3. **The Surgeon's Double Knot (Ask an Adult or Host if needed):**
   - Cross right cord over left, and loop through the circle **TWO times**!
   - Pull the cords firmly until the beads meet.
   - Cross left over right, loop through once, and pull tight.
4. **Hide the Knot:**
   - Trim the cord ends to 1/8" (about the thickness of two pennies).
   - Gently pull the nearest silicone bead right over the knot. Poof—the knot is hidden inside!
