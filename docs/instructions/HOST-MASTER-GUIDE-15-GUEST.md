## 3. Party Setup & Table Configuration

### Workspace Setup (10 Minutes Prior to Party)
- **Table Arrangement:** Seat guests around a central dining or craft table. 
- **Station Layout:** Place **one Bead Sorting Tray** and **one Craft Scissor** between every 7–8 guests. The anti-roll velvet surface keeps beads from rolling off the table onto the floor.
- **Seat Prep:** Lay one **Guest Step-by-Step Card** and one **Organza Favor Bag** at each guest's seat.
- **Letter Bead Station:** Keep the 60-piece alphabet letter collection in a shallow dish near the host seat. When guests design their projects, invite them to select up to 4–6 letters for their name or initials.

## 4. Project-by-Project Assembly Instructions

### Project 1: The Beadable Pen (Estimated Time: 10–15 min)

The beadable pen is the perfect icebreaker. It requires zero knots, zero glue, and delivers an immediate functional result.

```
       [Finial Top]
            | (Unscrew counter-clockwise)
            v
     [== Threaded Rod ==] <--- Thread 3-4 beads + 1 Theme Focal here!
     ====================
     [===== Pen Body ====]
            |
       (Twist tip to write)
```

1. **Unscrew the Finial:** Hold the barrel of the pen firmly in one hand and gently twist the round top finial counter-clockwise until it detaches from the threaded metal rod. Set the finial in your sorting tray.
2. **Design the Stack:** Select 1 Theme Focal bead and 3 to 4 Accent Beads. The total stack height should be approximately **2.0 to 2.2 inches**.
   - *Recommended Sequence:* `Small Accent Bead` -> `Large Theme Focal` -> `Faceted Spacer` -> `Accent Bead`.
3. **Slide Beads onto Rod:** Thread beads down the metal rod one by one. The silicone beads will glide smoothly with gentle pressure.
4. **Re-attach Finial:** Screw the top finial clockwise back onto the threaded rod.
   - *Key Host Tip:* Finger-tighten until snug. Do **not** use tools or overtighten. The silicone beads provide natural rubber friction against the finial, preventing it from unscrewing in backpacks.
5. **Test the Pen:** Twist the lower pen barrel clockwise to reveal the ballpoint tip. Scribble a test smile on the card!

### Project 2: The Backpack Charm / Keychain (Estimated Time: 15–20 min)

The backpack charm is durable, bold, and designed to clip directly onto school backpacks, sports duffels, or keychains.

1. **Hardware Overview:** Each guest receives one swivel lobster clasp connected to a split ring, and one heavy-duty charm drop cord.
2. **Attach Cord to Hardware (Lark's Head Knot):**
   - Fold the charm cord in half to create a loop at the top.
   - Push the folded loop through the small eyelet of the lobster clasp.
   - Take the two open tail ends of the cord and pull them through the loop.
   - Pull tails firmly to seat the knot tightly against the hardware.
3. **Thread the Charm:**
   - Hold both cord tails together as if they were a single strand.
   - Thread 1 accent bead over both tails to lock the top knot.
   - Slide on 1 Theme Focal bead, followed by 3–4 accent or alphabet beads.
4. **Tie the Bottom Stopper Knot:**
   - At the bottom of your bead stack, tie a firm double overhand knot.
   - Pull each cord strand separately in opposite directions to cinch the knot rock-solid.
   - Trim excess cord leaving 1/4" tail, or tuck the tails back up inside the bottom silicone bead's flexible cavity.

### Project 3: The Keepsake Stretch Bracelet (Estimated Time: 20–25 min)

This is the centerpiece project! Our 0.8mm high-durability elastic cord is engineered specifically for active hands.

```
  [Pre-Stretch Cord] ---> [Anchor Stopper] ---> [Bead Pattern] ---> [Surgeon's Knot]
   (Pull 3-4 times)         (Tape or Clip)        (14-16 beads)      (2x loop + 1x cinch)
```

1. **The Golden Rule: Pre-Stretch the Cord!**
   - Take the 12" pre-cut elastic cord.
   - Gently pull the cord between both hands 3 to 4 times, stretching it slightly.
   - *Why this matters:* Pre-stretching releases the factory tension, ensuring the bracelet stays true-to-size and never sags after being worn.
2. **Anchor the End:**
   - Fold a small piece of tape or pinch an anchor clip on one end of the cord about 2 inches from the tip so beads don't slide off while designing.
3. **String the Pattern:**
   - Typical wrist sizing:
     * Child / Youth (Ages 6–10): 13–14 beads (approx. 5.5 to 6.0 inches)
     * Teen / Adult (Ages 11+): 15–17 beads (approx. 6.5 to 7.0 inches)
   - Slide on your Theme Focal bead in the center, accented on each side with colorful silicone spacers and optional letter beads.
4. **The Master Surgeon's Knot (The Secret to Unbreakable Bracelets):**
   - Remove the anchor tape while holding both cord ends.
   - Bring both ends together. Cross the right cord over the left cord, then pass it through the center loop **TWICE** (not once like a shoe knot!).
   - Pull both ends smoothly to cinch the knot down snugly against the beads.
   - Cross left over right, pass through once, and pull firmly.
   - Give all four cord strands (the two main loops and two tails) a firm, gentle tug in opposite directions.
5. **Hide the Knot:**
   - Trim the cord tails to approximately 1/8 inch (3mm). Do not cut flush against the knot.
   - Gently pull the adjacent silicone bead over the knot. The flexible silicone hole will swallow the knot entirely, concealing it from sight and protecting it from friction!

## 5. Host Troubleshooting & Edge Cases

| Scenario / Issue | Immediate Host Fix |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Pen finial won't catch thread** | The bead stack is slightly too tall. Remove one thin spacer bead or gently compress the silicone beads downward with your fingers while turning the finial clockwise. |
| **Pen finial feels loose or spins** | The bead stack is too short, leaving exposed rod. Add one 10mm silicone spacer bead to take up the slack so the finial tightens against bead pressure. |
| **Bracelet elastic snapped during knotting** | The tester pulled with a sharp jerk instead of smooth, steady tension. Hand the guest a fresh 12" cord from your **Host Spare Supply Envelope**, remind them to pre-stretch gently, and help them cinch the Surgeon's knot slowly. |
| **Knot won't slide into bead hole** | Silicone beads have stretchable 2.5mm holes, whereas acrylic beads have rigid 1.5mm holes. Make sure the bead directly adjacent to the knot is a **silicone** bead or focal, not an acrylic spacer. |
| **Two guests want the same letter bead** | The 60-letter pool provides balanced popular initials. If two guests have names starting with the same letter, invite one to use their last-name initial or a matching focal symbol. |
| **Bead spills on the floor** | Use the anti-roll bead trays to keep beads corralled. If any piece is stepped on or lost, open the sealed **Host Spare Supply Envelope** for immediate replacements. |
